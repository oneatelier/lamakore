# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Lamakore is a shared workshop. We pledge to make participation
harassment-free for everyone: no demeaning language, no personal attacks,
no unwelcome advances, no bad-faith stonewalling in review.

## Our standards

- Assume the change is improvable and the author is reasonable.
- Critique the work, with evidence, in the open record (issues, ADRs,
  reports). Private vetoes do not exist here.
- Disclose conflicts of interest, especially around the `ee/` boundary.
- Respect the two gates: do not pressure anyone to mark [E] or [V]
  complete without the evidence.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the Maintainer of Record (see
`GOVERNANCE.md`). Reports are handled confidentially. Responses range
from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's
spaces, proportional to impact and history. Retaliation against reporters
is itself a violation.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, CI, releases, and
any official project channel.
